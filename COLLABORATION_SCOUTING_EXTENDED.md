## 📢 Outreach Rules (Reminder)

✅ **DO**:
- Contact only highly relevant people (score > 8)
- Personalize every message
- Offer a clear, small issue they can review
- Respect "do not contact" preferences
- Follow up after 2 weeks if no response

❌ **DON'T**:
- Mass-mention people on GitHub
- Spam notifications
- Automatically assign people to issues
- Contact without their consent
- Send generic messages
